Flyn

Flyn delivers curated fashion in minutes, bypassing traditional delivery wait times for a seamless shopping experience. We designed a versatile identity system built for scale, spanning digital interfaces, packaging, and rider gear.

ClientFlyn Tech Inc.
ReleaseJuly 2025
CategoryBrand Identity / E-commerce
The Challenge

Preserving Speed while Scaling Identity

Flyn required a unified, highly recognizable brand framework to articulate its core value proposition: instant fashion delivery. The challenge was building an agile, tiered logo architecture and visual system capable of working seamlessly across diverse touchpoints, from high-density mobile apps to physical delivery backpacks, without diluting brand integrity.

“Want it in minutes? Flyn it!”

Streamlined & Fast

Agile visual architecture tailored for high-speed digital touchpoints.

Modern & Bold

Distinctive color hierarchy driving consumer trust.

Consistent Scale

Strict grid rules ensuring flawless execution across digital and physical media.

Visual Identity

Crafting the Visual System

Our creative team defined a precise, scalable system anchored by custom typography (Debra & Poppins) and rigorous clear-space specifications, a 17px margin buffer rule applied consistently across every lockup.

We developed a tiered logo framework to adapt across various screen sizes and physical assets, eliminating brand misuse across marketing and product interfaces.

Logo construction grid, and in-store packaging locker mockup
Impact & Results

Results & Brand Impact

The refreshed visual system elevated Flyn's market presence, establishing a premium yet accessible brand identity. With standardized guidelines and standardized UI components, Flyn achieved complete visual consistency across all customer touchpoints, solidifying its position as a fast-fashion delivery pioneer.
